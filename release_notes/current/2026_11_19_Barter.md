## [Master] - 2026/11/19

### Fixed

- MINOR The Sharp-IB coupled Newton iteration could cycle without converging
  when a particle surface came to rest within a fraction of a nanometre of a
  mesh node: classifying the node as inside or outside the particle changed
  the discrete equations, and each classification moved the particle to the
  other side of the node. Support points within a small band around each
  particle surface (1e-2 times the cell size) now keep their previous
  inside/outside classification, so the coupled discrete problem always has a
  solution. Results can change only through nodes within that band.
